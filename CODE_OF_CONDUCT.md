# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Nucleus community respectful and constructive for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be direct and technical without being hostile.
- Assume good intent, but resolve ambiguity with evidence.
- Keep discussions focused on the project.
- Respect maintainers' decisions about scope, safety, and compatibility.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Repeatedly derailing technical discussions after maintainers ask to move on.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments, issues, pull requests, or discussions that violate this code. Maintainers may also temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

Report conduct issues through the private security contact listed in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) when public discussion would be inappropriate.
